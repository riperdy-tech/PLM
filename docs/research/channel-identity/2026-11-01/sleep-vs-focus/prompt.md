# Question: which of two YouTube music concepts earns more ad revenue?

Use only this brief. Ignore any memory or earlier chats. Neither option is preferred; decide on evidence alone.

## Context
- A new, faceless YouTube channel of original AI-generated instrumental music. It launches in late 2026 and is aiming for the most revenue it can sustain over several years.
- Two candidate concepts remain:
  - **A. Sleep music:** slow, wordless music for falling asleep and staying asleep, in 3–8 hour videos.
  - **B. Focus music:** steady instrumental electronic music for coding and deep work, in 90–120 minute videos.
- Target audience: English titles, aimed mainly at the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Germany.
- Reported, needs checking: from February 1, 2027, new channels need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 public watch hours in 12 months to join the YouTube Partner Program (currently 4,000 hours).

## The question
Which concept earns more YouTube ad revenue per month once mature, and which reaches monetization faster? Answer **A or B**.

## Cover
1. **Ads in the middle of videos (mid-rolls).** How do they affect each concept: listeners closing the video, coming back, and the number of ad impressions per view? YouTube says "meditation videos may not be suitable for mid-roll ads". Does that apply to sleep music in practice?
2. **Revenue per 1,000 views (RPM) and per watch hour** for each concept in the target countries. Use real creator data where it exists.
3. **What happens while a sleep listener is asleep.** Do ads keep playing and counting? Do "Are you still watching?" prompts stop playback? What about background play on mobile, and YouTube Premium revenue?
4. **Time to monetization:** how fast each concept builds watch hours toward the 8,000-hour bar.
5. **Back catalog:** do old videos keep earning, and how does that differ between the two?

## Rules
- Cite every number with who reported it and when. Label each one **measured**, **reported by a third party** or **your estimate**. If you can't find a figure, write "not found". Don't invent figures.
- Prefer evidence from channels started in 2024–2026.

## Output
1. **Answer:** A or B, in one paragraph.
2. **Evidence table:** each factor above, with A, B, the source, and the label.
3. **Rough monthly revenue for each concept** at 1M views a month, with your assumptions shown.
4. **The fact you are least sure of.**
