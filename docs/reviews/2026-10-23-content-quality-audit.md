# PLM content-quality and audience-performance audit — 2026-10-23

## Scope and evidence
Source review of the main PLM workspace: P1-P9 services, autonomous scripts, status, phase templates, tests. This is a code-level audit, not a live YouTube Studio retention-curve audit, visual review of competitor thumbnails, or listening-test measurement. In particular, a verified YouTube video ID in status is not proof that clickable player chapters appear.

## P0: stop false success, make chapter publication verifiable
1. P6 `metadata/compiler.py` produces chapter lines and `metadata/chapters.py` checks at least three markers, starts at zero, increasing order and >=10 s. P7 `preflight/gates.py` previously checked title/description length, not whether metadata matched the C6 chapter timeline. **Changed:** P7 Gate D rejects missing/invalid chapter sets and disagreement between description and timeline on >=10-minute or >=3-track packages. Tests cover failures. This is pre-publication validation, not confirmation YouTube displayed chapters.
2. YouTube treats description timestamps as player chapter inputs; embedding FFMetadata chapters into MP4 alone cannot establish that YouTube's player exposes them. After upload, reconcile video processing state and examine its actual public watch page/eligible account to verify clickable chapters; do not mark chapter_verified until confirmed.
3. `scripts/autonomous_loop_worker.py` reads a fixed video, cover, description, and existing video ID, does not generate/upload on the claimed steps, and may send a fixed experiment ID as a MATURE 28-day reward based on a 7-day analytics query. **Changed:** run_cycle raises immediately so this unsafe legacy path cannot pollute P2 or masquerade as a new publication. Restore only via a fresh pipeline artifact, P5 gate, authorized P7 receipt, persisted lineage and publication time, and genuine maturity calculation.
4. `scripts/produce_full_playlist.py` bypasses the guarded P7 engine via direct RealYouTubeClient upload; its workflow has static clip IDs, predefined similar prompts and titles. Deprecate this publishing route or route it through ExternalWriteGuard + content gates. It lacks a post-upload check.
5. P7 `client/real.py` previously announced JPEG for all cover uploads even when P6 generated PNG. **Changed:** PNG and JPEG content types are inferred from signatures; malformed payloads are rejected before HTTP.
